What runs on your PC

Everything here reads your log file (and Zeal, if you use it). None of it needs an account.

Damage and tank meter

Every fight from your own log, with pets, history and copy-to-chat.

Triggers that speak

Text, voice and countdowns. Import your GINA or EQLogParser triggers.

Spell and buff timers

The full spell list ships with Mimic, so timers know their durations.

Charm and pet trackers

Charm breaks, re-charm countdown and your pet's buffs.

Bard melody and tick timer

Your song rotation and the server tick, at a glance.

Zeal, set up for you

Installs and updates Zeal and its target rings.

What needs a guild server

These combine what several players' Mimics see, so they need a guild running the server. In local mode they say so instead of showing anything.

Mob Info

A mob's stats, loot and spells.

Raid buff queue

Who in the raid is missing what.

Extended target and heal chains

Everyone's targets and casts, shared live.

Get going

  1. Download and run the installer. It is not code-signed, so Windows may warn you. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. On first launch, choose Run local-only for now. Mimic stops asking you to sign in.
  3. In game, type /log on. Mimic finds your EverQuest folder and starts reading.
  4. Turn on the overlays you want from the dashboard. Unlock them to move and size them over the game.

The installer is named Wolf Pack Mimic, after the Project Quarm guild that builds it. You do not need to be in the guild.

The only thing Mimic contacts on its own is GitHub, to check for updates to itself and to Zeal.
