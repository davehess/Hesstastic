mimic

EverQuest overlays that read your log and never upload it. For Project Quarm. Free, Windows.

Download for Windows Opens the releases page.

On your PC

Read from your own log, and Zeal if you have it.

  • Damage and tank meter
  • Triggers with voice and countdowns
  • GINA and EQLogParser trigger import
  • Spell and buff timers
  • Charm and pet trackers
  • Bard melody, tick timer, HUD
  • Zeal install, updates, target rings

Needs a guild server

Combines what several players see. In local mode these say so.

  • Mob Info stats, loot and spells
  • Raid buff queue
  • Extended target
  • Heal chains
  • Raid parses on a website

Privacy, exactly

  • Reads: your EverQuest log file, and Zeal's data if Zeal is running.
  • Sends: nothing. In local mode no fight, chat or character leaves your PC.
  • Contacts: GitHub, to check for updates to Mimic and to Zeal. Nothing else on its own.

Setup

  1. Run the installer. It is not code-signed, so Windows may warn: More info, then Run anyway.
  2. On first launch choose Run local-only for now.
  3. In game, type /log on.
  4. Turn overlays on from the dashboard, then unlock them to place them.

Questions

Why is the installer called Wolf Pack Mimic?

Wolf Pack is the Project Quarm guild that builds it. You do not need to be in the guild to use it.

Do I need Zeal?

No. Everything that reads the log works without it. Zeal adds live HP, the tick timer and target details, and Mimic can install it for you.

I am in Wolf Pack. What changes?

Sign in from Settings and the guild features turn on.
