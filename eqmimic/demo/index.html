A raid night with Mimic, start to finish.

Everything below runs on numbers from one real raid night in Ssraeshza Temple: six bosses, forty-odd raiders. Every name is invented, and so is the guild, Lantern Watch. Nothing here is live, and nothing you click is saved.

Mimic on its own reads your log and draws the overlays, with nothing sent anywhere. The guild site and the officer pages need a guild to run the server; they are here so you can see what that adds.

1 · DURING THE FIGHT

On your screen

The damage meter with your own row always in view, countdowns for the things that matter, a callout in the middle when the boss turns on you, and the target's card: what it hits for, what it resists, what it can't be.

2 · AFTER THE FIGHT

Your control panel

Mimic's own window, on your PC. Your fights, your triggers, which overlays show, and the one switch that matters most: local mode, where nothing leaves your machine.

3 · THE NIGHT

Your guild's site guild server

Every raider's Mimic sees part of each fight; the site merges them into one parse per boss. Pick a boss to see the whole raid.
